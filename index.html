Hi! I’m Yichen (Ruby) Pan, a PhD student in Business Technologies at Carnegie Mellon University's Tepper School of Business.

My research interests lie at the intersection of information systems, marketing, and machine learning. Broadly, I am interested in how AI and digital platforms shape consumer behavior, social interactions, and decision-making.

Before Carnegie Mellon, I earned an MS in Marketing Science from Columbia Business School (2024), and degrees in Management Information Systems and Mathematics from the University of Texas at Austin (2023).

Research

My interests include artificial intelligence, digital platforms, user-generated content, consumer analytics, social networks, and causal inference.
