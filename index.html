Hi! I’m Yichen Pan (潘怡辰), a PhD student in Business Technologies at Carnegie Mellon University's Tepper School of Business.

My research interests lie at the intersection of the economics of business technologies and marketing. Broadly, I am interested in how AI and emergent technologies shape consumer behavior, social interactions, and decision-making.

Prior to joining Carnegie Mellon, I earned an MS in Marketing Science from Columbia Business School, and degrees in Management Information Systems and Mathematics from the University of Texas at Austin.
